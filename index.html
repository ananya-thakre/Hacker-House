Before you ask

Questions worth answering upfront.

Is my photo, email or phone number uploaded anywhere?

No. The card is drawn on your device with the Canvas API. Your details never leave the browser tab unless you press Save or Post — and even then, only the finished image goes to your gallery or to X's own composer.

Why do you ask for email and phone if nothing is uploaded?

They print on the Builder ID as your on-record contact, the way a real event pass would. We validate the format so your card never ships with a broken email or a phone number missing a digit — that's the only reason for the check.

Does this ID get me a seat at Hacker House Goa?

No — and it doesn't pretend to. Seats are decided through the official shortlisting process on Devfolio. This tool is for the #FrameInGoa task itself.

Can I put my whole team on one card?

Yes. Switch the format to Team Frame and choose Duo or Trio — each teammate gets their own photo, name and stack.
